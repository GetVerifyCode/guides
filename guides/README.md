# GetVerifyCode Guides

Practical notes on SMS one-time codes, delivery failures, and how temporary numbers fit into verification.

These pages are written for engineers, QA, and product teams who ship phone checks. They stay vendor-neutral where it matters, and link to [GetVerifyCode](https://getverifycode.com/) when a temporary inbound number is useful.
